# Cao Vũ Kỷ Nguyên Translation Harness

Translate Chinese futuristic sci-fi high-martial arts and evolution prose into sharp, readable Vietnamese.

## Style

- Keep martial arts cultivation, level-ups, and combat descriptions clear, kinetic, and concrete.
- Keep future technology, space, and evolution/system terminology consistent.
- Prefer direct Vietnamese phrasing for action beats.
- Keep specialized abilities, gene levels, factions, titles, and equipment terms stable once chosen.

## Formatting

- Line 1 of each Vietnamese chapter must be `Chương <global_index>: <Title>`.
- Line 2 must be empty.
- Preserve paragraph boundaries.
- Remove author notes, vote requests (e.g. cầu nguyệt phiếu, cầu đề cử), unrelated updates, and translator notes.
