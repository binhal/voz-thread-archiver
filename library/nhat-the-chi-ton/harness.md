# Nhất Thế Chi Tôn Translation Harness

Translate Chinese xianxia, martial arts, and Daoist/Buddhist fantasy prose into polished, natural, and elegant Vietnamese.

## Style

- Balance Hán-Việt and Thuần Việt.
- Keep martial arts techniques, cultivation realms, Buddhist/Daoist concepts, and epic terminology in elegant Hán-Việt (e.g. Khai Khiếu, Ngoại Cảnh, Pháp Thân, Thần Chưởng, Thiền Tông, v.v.).
- Translate narrative descriptions and common verbs into smooth, natural Vietnamese.
- Avoid rigid word-for-word translation.
- Keep the rhythm dynamic, fast-paced, and immersive.
- Use standard Vietnamese idioms where they fit naturally.

## Character Voice

- Mạnh Kỳ (孟奇) / Chân Định (真定): The main character. Use `hắn` or `cậu` in narration. He is witty, humorous, and sometimes sarcastic. Use standard polite pronouns with seniors and comfortable, casual pronouns with friends/peers.
- Giang Chỉ Vy (江芷微): Sword prodigy from Tẩy Kiếm Các. Spirited and determined. Narration uses `nàng` or `cô`.
- Cố Tiểu Tang (顾小桑): Maiden of La Giáo. Mysterious, playful, and lethal. She often refers to Mạnh Kỳ as `tướng công` (husband) or `Chi Chi` (Zhi Zhi).
- Chân Tuệ (真慧): Mạnh Kỳ's simple-minded but pure and talented junior monk brother.

## Formatting

- Line 1 of each Vietnamese chapter must be `Chương <global_index>: <Title>`.
- Line 2 must be empty.
- Preserve paragraph boundaries.
- Remove author notes, vote requests, unrelated updates, and translator notes.
